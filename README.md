## Assembly Hall Admin Console

**An internal data visualization tool designed to support data-driven decision making around volunteer coverage and maintenance needs. To view the app, enter any credentials for username and password**

[View the Assembly Hall Prototype →](https://stroke-quartz-93237691.figma.site)

The dashboard brings volunteer availability, crew coverage, and historical trends into one place, allowing administrators to explore questions such as:

- Are current volunteer numbers meeting maintenance needs?
- Which crews or months have the greatest coverage gaps?
- How does current coverage compare with previous years?
- Where might additional volunteer capacity be needed?
- When new volunteers become available, which crews could benefit most from additional support?

The goal is not to make these decisions automatically, but to provide the information needed to support informed organizational decisions.

**Key Features**
>Coverage Dashboard — Visualizes volunteer needs, availability, and coverage by crew.

>Monthly & Yearly Trends — Compares coverage over time to identify patterns and potential gaps.

>Crew Calendars — Provides a detailed view of availability for individual crews.

>Volunteer Data — Allows administrators to drill down to crew-level volunteer list and volunteer data.

>Quarterly Reporting — Provides a way to summarize and share coverage information.

**Design Process**

I began by sketching the different views by hand, focusing on how an administrator would move from a high-level overview into more detailed information.

The interface was then developed into an interactive administrative experience using AI driven development, centered around:

**Overview → Explore → Investigate → Understand**

*Project Background*

**This project is an expansion of a previous application I developed called Volunteer Availability.**

Volunteer Availability focuses on the individual volunteer, providing a crew-specific calendar where volunteers can indicate when they are available. Crews can use this information to invite volunteers and schedule maintenance tasks.

The Assembly Hall Admin Console takes that information and looks at it from an organizational perspective—turning individual availability data into a broader view of coverage, capacity, and potential volunteer needs.

Together, the projects explore how scheduling data can move from individual participation to organizational insight.
